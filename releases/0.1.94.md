# Cindx 0.1.94

This release closes the controlled Auto-to-Pro-to-Auto learning loop. It adds
replayable Pro-to-Auto self-distillation without copying Pro's larger budget or
topology into the foreground Auto runtime. It does not claim a measured
intelligence uplift: the release gate observed no provider-backed arena runs.

## Pro-to-Auto self-distillation

- Only the active project-scoped frozen Pro champion can become a teacher. Its
  attestation binds the ordinary Pro evidence, Auto-to-Pro transfer evidence,
  frozen profile, and complete source lineage that qualified it.
- Distillation derives a deterministic Auto-compatible child from the stable
  Auto parent and the learned Pro delta. It transfers at most two compatible
  genes and preserves Auto limits for topology, model turns, tool calls,
  parallelism, synthesis, and verification. Pro directives, budgets, and raw
  genomes are never copied wholesale.
- The direction is explicitly versioned as `pro_to_auto_distillation`.
  Teacher, parent, child, dataset, cohort, attempt, receipt, and flattened
  ancestor digests remain independently replayable. Legacy or incomplete
  lineage fails closed.
- Teacher and ancestor cases, and objectives with the same canonical digest,
  cannot enter the fresh distillation train or holdout cohort. Missing typed
  manifests, cross-split reuse, treatment failures, timeouts, permission
  denials, and malformed terminal state remain in the failure boundary.
- Distillation uses an independent project-and-track request identity. The
  durable outbox does not scan or dispatch while a foreground Agent run is
  active, and the bounded background campaign remains cancellable and
  recoverable.
- A passing matched train/holdout gate starts a 10 percent Auto canary. Fresh
  live evidence advances 10 to 25 to 50 percent and then freezes the promoted
  Auto profile. Safety, quality, latency, token, gate, or lineage regression
  rolls back to the prior stable snapshot.

## Bounded review findings closed

- Canonical replay now projects the distillation method only onto the child;
  the stable Auto parent keeps its original provenance.
- The staged canary is reachable after the finite matched campaign: later
  stages require fresh live evidence and revalidate the frozen matched gate,
  rather than waiting for an already completed campaign to create more offline
  comparisons.
- Foreground activity prevents every new distillation outbox scan, avoiding
  global event-store and CPU contention with an active Auto run.
- The read model and worker responsibilities remain under their existing
  structure budgets; no budget was relaxed.
